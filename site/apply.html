Human by Design is building a small, hand-picked group of readers in English and Arabic. People come to us after meeting their chart for free, and they're ready for a reading. We handle the bookings and the payments, and pay you after each session.

What we look for

  • Real training in Human Design, and charts read for real clients
  • Readings that explain, never label: warm, plain-spoken and practical
  • Respect for the system as taught by Ra Uru Hu, and honesty where schools differ
  • Arabic readers especially welcome

How it works: you apply, we look at your answers and a sample of your work, and we meet you on a short call. If it's a fit, we agree your price and the platform fee before your profile goes live.
